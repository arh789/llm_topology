Phase B found a **coherent but non-monotonic semantic evolution across transformer layers**.

The strongest positive-side pattern is a persistent lineage that moves from **operational information/task structure → inquiry/analysis → development/transformation → analytical/professional information activity → functional information-processing workflow**.

```text id="g9r2ck"
domain organisation
    ↓
operational organisation
    ↓
inquiry / characterisation
    ↓
development / transformation
    ↓
analytical / professional information activity
    ↓
information-processing → state-change → reporting
```

The positive side therefore becomes generally **more abstract and more integrated**, although it repeatedly splits and recombines. Biological/health and professional/interface structures often reappear as separate parents, then merge back into the dominant process spine.

The strongest negative-side finding is earlier stabilisation around two persistent structures:

```text id="5magu5"
T-C
characterisation
+ regulation / constraint
+ adverse evaluation
+ controlled / destructive change

T-A
authority
+ territory
+ institutional / social order
```

The negative side therefore converges earlier and remains structurally more stable than the positive side.

Other major findings:

- **Biological/health semantics persist throughout**, but progressively lose independent parent status and become satellites of broader analytical/process structures.
- Early **social/institutional/geographic organisation** is gradually absorbed into professional/organisational information activity.
- A **mediated professional/interface branch** repeatedly splits from and rejoins the dominant positive lineage.
- Material environments and pathology persist on the negative side, but usually as secondary or irreducible structures rather than stable independent parents.
- No high-confidence **side change** was found; similar structures can coexist on positive and negative sides rather than migrate between them.

The largest Phase-B semantic discontinuities were:

1. **S00→S01** — material/energy domain organisation replaced by contextual information/social/living organisation.
2. **S14→S15** — major positive compression into one inquiry/analytical structure.
3. **S16→S17** — health loses independent status and merges into a broader developmental/process structure.
4. **S26→S27** — major late integration into one analytical/professional information parent covering **72.1%** of semantic-eligible positive nodes.
5. **S30→S31** — late re-differentiation into analytical/evidential versus professional/information-production structures.
6. **S31→S32** — terminal recombination into an explicit functional workflow.

The most compact Phase-B conclusion is:

```text id="abdt1n"
early layers
= organise domains

middle layers
= organise operations and constraints

late layers
= organise relations between operations

final state
= organise a functional workflow
```

So Phase B's core result is not merely that topics change across layers. It is that the **type of organisation changes**: from domain-based semantics toward increasingly relational, operational and process-based structure.
