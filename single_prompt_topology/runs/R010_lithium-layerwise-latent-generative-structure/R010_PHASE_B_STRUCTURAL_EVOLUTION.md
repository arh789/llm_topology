# R010 Phase B — cross-state structural evolution

## Status

**PHASE B STRUCTURAL EVOLUTION ANALYSIS: COMPLETE**

Phase B consumes the frozen, independently reconstructed Phase-A states after the Phase-A completeness verification passed.

This analysis does **not** alter the Phase-A state files. It infers correspondence among state-local parent candidates and asks how those higher-order semantic structures evolve across States 00–32.

It does not compare the semantic trajectory with R008 measured Jaccard/ARI/topology quantities; that remains Phase C. It also does not assign SDA-3 roles, identify an optimal layer or infer curvature/coherence.

## Method

Correspondence is inferred from:

1. semantic identity of each frozen state-local parent;
2. its child-community functions/domains;
3. its relations to other parents and irreducible structures in the same state;
4. semantic-eligible parent coverage;
5. adjacent-state plausibility without requiring identical local IDs or labels.

A cross-state family is retained only when a sequence of independently inferred parents shares a discriminative organising relation. Similar wording alone is not enough.

Allowed trajectory descriptors follow the R010 protocol:

- persistent;
- strengthening / weakening;
- broadening / narrowing;
- split / merge;
- appearance / disappearance;
- reappearance;
- side change;
- uncertain correspondence.

## Executive result

A coherent but non-monotonic semantic trajectory is present.

The strongest result is an **asymmetry between the positive and negative sides**:

- The **positive side remains plastic**. It begins as a set of domain-specific structures, then repeatedly reorganises around an increasingly abstract operational/inquiry/process spine. Biological, social and professional structures alternately remain independent, become satellites, merge into the spine, and reappear.
- The **negative side stabilises much earlier**. By the middle layers it repeatedly reconstructs a control architecture organised around **characterisation / regulation / adverse evaluation / controlled change**, accompanied by a second structure organised around **authority / territory / institutional-social order**.

The overall semantic development is therefore not simply "more topics" or "fewer topics". It is a shift from **domain organisation** toward **relational/process organisation**, with progressively greater integration on the positive side and persistent two-axis organisation on the negative side.

## Global correspondence families

### Positive family T+O — operational → inquiry → process → information-workflow spine

**Confidence: High**

This is the strongest positive correspondence.

Representative sequence:

```text
S01  information systems / maintenance
 ↓
S03–S08 information access, task, work, transactions
 ↓
S09–S14 operational interaction → investigation → coordinated inquiry
 ↓
S15–S17 inquiry / interaction → transformation / developmental process
 ↓
S18–S26 development / measurement / quantitative inquiry
 ↓
S27–S30 analytical / professional / technical information activity
 ↓
S31 analytical-evidential + professional-information split
 ↓
S32 information-processing → state-change → reporting workflow
```

The family changes in content but preserves an organising relation: **acting on, analysing, transforming, coordinating or communicating information/system state**.

Its dominant positive semantic-eligible coverage rises substantially across the run. The mean largest positive-parent coverage is:

- S01–S08: **34.5%**
- S09–S14: **43.3%**
- S15–S17: **51.2%**
- S18–S26: **44.3%**
- S27–S31: **61.5%**

The late dip/re-expansion reflects repeated splitting and re-merging rather than disappearance.

**Trajectory:** persistent → broadening → abstraction → intermittent split/merge → late integration → terminal functional reorganisation.

### Positive family T+B — biological / health / living systems

**Confidence: High for semantic persistence; Moderate for parent-level continuity**

This family is present from the input embedding onward:

- S00 biomedical/pharmacological substances;
- S01–S08 biological/health/living or biomedical/molecular parents;
- S09 becomes irreducible rather than parent-level;
- S10–S14 repeatedly returns as a retained parent;
- S15 loses parent status;
- S16 reappears as a health/psychosocial/biomedical parent;
- S17–S18 again becomes irreducible/mixed;
- S19 reappears as biomedical health/lived wellbeing;
- S20–S22 becomes an irreducible satellite;
- S23 reappears as embodied wellbeing/biomedical health;
- S24–S27 becomes mixed/secondary;
- S28 reappears as embodied health/lived wellbeing;
- S29–S32 remains present but mostly mixed or secondary.

This is not disappearance of biological semantics. It is a **loss of independent generative status**. As the transformer deepens, biological/health material increasingly attaches to the dominant analytical/process architecture rather than forming its own parent.

**Trajectory:** persistent semantic domain → weakening independent parent status → recurrent reappearance → late satellite integration.

### Positive family T+S — social / institutional / geographic organisation

**Confidence: Moderate to High**

Clear parent-level forms occur across S01–S09:

- social/institutional organisation;
- geopolitical/civic organisation;
- geographic/social-cultural identity;
- political/geographic/organisational-economic activity.

After S09 it stops appearing consistently as a distinct parent. Its functions migrate into:

- organised task/professional activity;
- inquiry/communication;
- enterprise/research;
- mediated professional information production.

This is best interpreted as a **merge into the operational/professional spine**, not simple disappearance.

**Trajectory:** early persistence → narrowing as independent social/geographic structure → merge into T+O/T+M.

### Positive family T+M — mediated professional / interface / information-production branch

**Confidence: High after S18; Moderate before S18**

This family is a recurrent branch of T+O rather than a fully independent lineage.

Clear retained-parent appearances:

- S18 mediated learning/communication/software-interface systems;
- S20 mediated creative/professional activity;
- S21 mediated social/creative/interface systems;
- S24 mediated creative/research/professional activity;
- S26 digital interface/information-production systems;
- S31 mediated professional/enterprise/information-production activity.

At S22, S25 and S27–S30, similar material is absorbed into the dominant T+O parent or remains irreducible. At S31 it splits cleanly from analytical/evidential interpretation before S32 recombines the functional pieces into a workflow.

**Trajectory:** appearance → repeated merge/reappearance → late split → terminal recombination.

### Negative family T-C — control / characterisation / adverse change backbone

**Confidence: Very High**

This is the strongest cross-state correspondence in Phase B.

Early states contain partially separated precursors:

- S01 transformation/interpretive constraint;
- S02 prohibition/harm plus scientific controlled transformation;
- S03 adverse/prohibited harm plus scientific/clinical analysis;
- S04 analytical description plus evaluation/regulation/state change;
- S05 analysis/clinical characterisation plus adversity/conflict;
- S08 and S09 still split assessment from adverse/destructive change.

By S10 these strands are reconstructed as one parent:

- evaluative characterisation / constraint / destructive change.

From S11 onward the same higher-order relation repeatedly reappears with only local emphasis changes:

- quantitative characterisation;
- regulation;
- administrative configuration;
- adverse evaluation/judgement;
- prohibition;
- controlled change;
- destructive/state change.

Representative late sequence:

```text
S17 regulatory characterisation / administration
 ↓
S18–S21 administrative characterisation + adverse constraint/change
 ↓
S22–S27 regulatory/adverse intervention + administrative state characterisation
 ↓
S28–S31 regulatory characterisation + adverse/prohibitive/controlled change
 ↓
S32 structural characterisation + extreme evaluation + destructive-state change
```

Dominant negative-parent semantic-eligible coverage is already high by the middle layers and remains near half of the semantic-eligible negative population through most late states.

**Trajectory:** early split precursors → merge/consolidation around S10 → strong persistence → local broadening/narrowing → terminal reformulation.

### Negative family T-A — authority / territory / institutional-social order

**Confidence: High**

A distinct authority/order family becomes clear by S03–S04 and persists through nearly the entire remaining run:

- institutional authority/public-social roles;
- institutional/familial/territorial order;
- civic/territorial authority/social order;
- institutional/territorial/political authority;
- territorial identity/institutional-mythic authority;
- ceremonial/civic/institutional authority at S32.

This family is typically the **secondary negative parent**, while T-C is dominant.

Its semantic-eligible coverage is generally smaller, often roughly 15–30%, but it is much more stable in identity than many positive-side parents.

**Trajectory:** appearance in early transformer → persistent secondary architecture → gradual narrowing toward authority/territory → terminal ceremonial/civic reformulation.

### Negative family T-M — material / built / technical environment

**Confidence: Moderate**

Material environments are highly persistent semantically but unstable as a parent.

They appear as:

- mixed built/weapons/objects communities early;
- part of authority/conflict parents at S06, S08, S10;
- separate material/technical infrastructure parent at S14;
- irreducible material-setting satellites at S15–S16;
- merged into authority/material parents at S17, S19–S20 and S25;
- predominantly irreducible satellites again from S27 onward.

This is best interpreted as a **supporting context repeatedly attaching to T-A and T-C**, not a stable independent parent lineage.

**Trajectory:** recurrent attachment → occasional parent formation → repeated split back to irreducible setting.

### Negative family T-P — scientific / clinical / pathology organisation

**Confidence: High for semantic persistence; Moderate for parent-level continuity**

Scientific/clinical analysis is parent-level in several early states:

- S02 scientific/biomedical analysis;
- S03 scientific/clinical analysis;
- S04–S05 analytical/clinical characterisation;
- S08–S09 scientific/clinical/technical analysis.

Later, cardiovascular/cytokine/pathology communities remain highly recurrent but are usually irreducible satellites to T-C rather than independent parents.

The trajectory parallels positive T+B: the domain persists, but its **structural autonomy decreases**.

**Trajectory:** early independent parent → partial merge with characterisation/control → late irreducible pathology satellite.

## Abstraction, differentiation and integration

### Does the architecture become more abstract?

**Yes, strongly.**

The positive sequence shifts from concrete domain structures toward increasingly relational descriptions:

```text
materials / electrochemistry / biomedical substances
        ↓
information / work / social organisation / living systems
        ↓
investigation / inquiry / interaction
        ↓
development / transformation / measurement
        ↓
analytical / professional information activity
        ↓
information-processing / state-change / reporting workflow
```

The negative side follows a parallel abstraction:

```text
harm / scientific analysis / institutional roles
        ↓
evaluation / regulation / characterisation
        ↓
administrative control / adverse judgement / controlled change
        ↓
structural characterisation / extreme evaluation / destructive change
```

The later parents are less about *what domain the tokens belong to* and more about *what relation or operation organises them*.

### Does it become more integrated?

**Positive side: yes, with repeated temporary re-differentiation.**

Average retained positive parent count falls from **3.13** in S01–S08 to **1.40** in S27–S31, while the mean largest positive-parent semantic coverage rises from **34.5%** to **61.5%**.

This is strong evidence of semantic compression into fewer higher-order structures.

**Negative side: integration happens earlier, then stabilises.**

From S15 onward the negative side is almost uniformly reconstructed as two parents:

1. control/characterisation/adverse change;
2. authority/territorial/social order.

The negative side therefore looks less like continuing integration and more like **early convergence followed by persistence**.

### Does it become more differentiated?

Locally, yes.

The run is not monotonic. Health and mediated-professional structures repeatedly split from and rejoin the dominant positive spine. The late S30→S31 split is the clearest example of renewed differentiation immediately before the final S32 merge.

## Side-change analysis

**No high-confidence side change is identified.**

Several semantic domains occur on both sides — especially biomedical/clinical material, institutional structures and technical systems — but they frequently coexist rather than migrate exclusively from one side to the other.

Therefore apparent semantic similarity across signs is not sufficient evidence of a side-change trajectory.

## Strongest Phase-B claims

1. **A persistent positive operational/inquiry/process spine exists from the first contextual layer through final normalisation.**
2. **That spine becomes progressively more abstract and generally more integrated, although it repeatedly splits into health and mediated-professional branches.**
3. **Biological/health semantics persist across the entire network but lose independent parent status in later layers, increasingly becoming satellites of broader analytical/process structures.**
4. **Early positive social/institutional/geographic organisation is progressively absorbed into professional/organisational information activity.**
5. **The negative side converges earlier than the positive side on a stable control architecture: characterisation/regulation/adverse evaluation/controlled change.**
6. **A second negative authority/territorial/social-order architecture persists alongside that control backbone through most layers.**
7. **Material environments and pathology remain persistent semantic satellites but have unstable parent-level status.**
8. **The final normalised state is not merely a stronger version of S31: it reorganises the positive structure into an explicit functional workflow.**

## Competing interpretation

The strongest alternative is that T+O is too broad and retroactively merges several independent families:

- information/task systems;
- inquiry/analysis;
- developmental/quantitative processes;
- professional information production.

Under this interpretation, the transformer repeatedly replaces one dominant positive structure with another rather than evolving a single spine.

The preferred correspondence is stronger because:

1. adjacent states repeatedly preserve the same operational relation even when vocabulary domains change;
2. temporary branches repeatedly rejoin the same dominant structure;
3. the sequence from task → inquiry → developmental analysis → information workflow is relationally continuous rather than merely lexically similar;
4. late states explicitly combine functions that earlier states represented separately.

Nevertheless, the correspondence should be treated as a **high-confidence structural lineage, not identity of a single measured feature**.

A second alternative is that T-C contains two persistent negative lineages — neutral characterisation/analysis and adverse/prohibitive control — that repeatedly merge and split. The Phase-A evidence supports this especially before S10 and at S13–S14. Phase B therefore treats **early T-C as a converging family with two precursor strands**, not as a fully unified structure from the first layer.

## Phase-B answers to the protocol questions

### Which parent structures correspond across states?

High-confidence correspondences:

- positive T+O operational/inquiry/process spine;
- positive T+B biological/health/living domain;
- negative T-C control/characterisation/adverse-change backbone;
- negative T-A authority/territorial/social-order backbone.

Moderate/high correspondences:

- positive T+S early social/institutional/geographic family;
- positive T+M mediated professional/interface branch;
- negative T-M material/environmental context;
- negative T-P scientific/clinical/pathology family.

### Which persist, strengthen, weaken, split or merge?

- **T+O:** persists, broadens, strengthens in late coverage, repeatedly splits/merges.
- **T+B:** persists semantically but weakens as an independent parent; reappears episodically.
- **T+S:** persists early, then merges into professional/operational organisation.
- **T+M:** appears and repeatedly reappears as a branch of T+O.
- **T-C:** precursor strands merge by approximately S10; then strongly persists.
- **T-A:** appears early and strongly persists as secondary negative architecture.
- **T-M:** recurrently merges with/splits from T-A and T-C.
- **T-P:** persists semantically but weakens from parent to satellite.

### Does the architecture become more abstract, differentiated or integrated?

- **More abstract:** yes.
- **More integrated:** strongly on the positive side; earlier and then stable on the negative side.
- **More differentiated:** locally and cyclically, not monotonically.

### Is there a coherent developmental trajectory?

**Yes.**

The most compact description is:

```text
DOMAIN ORGANISATION
      ↓
OPERATIONAL ORGANISATION
      ↓
INQUIRY / CHARACTERISATION
      ↓
DEVELOPMENT / TRANSFORMATION
      ↓
ANALYTICAL / PROFESSIONAL INFORMATION ACTIVITY
      ↓
FUNCTIONAL INFORMATION-PROCESSING WORKFLOW
```

Against that positive trajectory, the negative side converges on:

```text
CHARACTERISE
   + CONSTRAIN / REGULATE
   + EVALUATE ADVERSELY
   + CHANGE / REMOVE / PROHIBIT

alongside

AUTHORITY
   + TERRITORY
   + SOCIAL / INSTITUTIONAL ORDER
```

## Phase-B boundary

Phase B establishes a semantic structural trajectory from the frozen independent Phase-A reconstructions.

It does **not** establish whether these semantic discontinuities correspond to measured changes in R008 network topology.

The next authorised stage is **Phase C**, which should compare the Phase-B discontinuities and trajectories against pre-existing R008 measurements such as membership Jaccard, edge Jaccard, community ARI, population size, community count and intercommunity organisation.

No SDA-3 role, optimal-layer judgement or curvature/coherence inference is made in this artifact.
