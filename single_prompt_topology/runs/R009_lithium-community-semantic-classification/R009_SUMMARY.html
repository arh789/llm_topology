Evidence and limits

What is represented

  • 33 recorded states from input embedding to final normalisation.
  • 392 positive-head and 400 negative-tail community instances.
  • Broad identity, top 10 graph-degree representatives, structure type, confidence and concise evidence for every community.

What remains unresolved

  • Whether each label describes a genuinely coherent semantic structure.
  • Which communities correspond across states despite changing local IDs.
  • Whether any stable roles map to C, S, A, HCU or E.
  • Which states expose those relationships most clearly.
