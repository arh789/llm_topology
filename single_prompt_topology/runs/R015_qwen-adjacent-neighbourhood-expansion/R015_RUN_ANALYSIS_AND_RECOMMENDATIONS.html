Interpretation

R015 provides evidence against the narrow explanation that R014 merely decomposed its four literal prompt concepts. Every original seed was replaced, yet all six higher-order families reappeared after independent reconstruction. The dominant positive sensing/place organisation and the negative spatial/group/process organisation also recur.

The result does not establish a global transformer ontology or a causal internal mechanism. It establishes local semantic continuity under a controlled movement of the observation window.

Recommendation

Proceed next to a distant-region replication with the R014/R015 measurement and independent-reconstruction methodology held fixed. The purpose should be to test whether the same kind of organisation survives outside this semantic neighbourhood. Do not compare with SDA-3 yet; preserve SDA-3 as a downstream hypothesis that can fail against a larger independently generated map.
