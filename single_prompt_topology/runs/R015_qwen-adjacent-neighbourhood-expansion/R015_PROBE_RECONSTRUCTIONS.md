# R015 independent probe reconstructions

## Purpose

Record the first-pass semantic reconstruction of R015 P1-P4 before comparison
with R014. The reconstruction is based on the completed R015 measured community,
community-edge, transition and permutation-invariance artefacts. R014 parent
labels were not used as classification targets.

The broad labels below were assigned from recurrent R015 community content.
After the four probe-local reconstructions were complete, compatible families
were matched across probes.

For the quantitative downstream audit, a family was considered present when
strong lexical indicators appeared in a community's representative or
most-extreme tokens. A relation was counted over S06-S27 when two families
occurred in the same community or in communities joined by an observed
community edge. These support values are semantic diagnostics over the
published community summaries, not original model measurements.

## P1 — sensor | apartment | line

Held out: `sessions`.

### Positive-side parents

**P1-A — sensing / instrumentation**

Sensor, sensors, laser, signal, electrodes and related apparatus vocabulary
recur across the six orders. The family is present in every audited S06-S27
positive network.

**P1-B — built place / locality**

Apartment, shop, house and named-place material form a persistent positive
locality branch. The family is present in approximately 95% of audited positive
networks.

**P1-C — spatial / measurement structure**

Line/area/space/field/size and related structural or measurement vocabulary
appears on both sides, but is more persistent on the negative side in R015.

**P1-F — reporting / information**

Report, newsletter, view, output/file and related representation vocabulary
recurs as a secondary positive branch.

**P1-G — modification / transformation**

Enlargement, decreasing, transformations and removal vocabulary recurs on the
positive side. This is important because the corresponding broader process
family also has a distinct negative-side form.

### Negative-side parents

**P1-D — organised group / activity**

Clubs, sessions, activities, FIFA, tennis and related group/activity vocabulary
form a persistent negative branch.

This is a held-out reconstruction. Literal `sessions` appears in community
summaries in all six P1 permutations across 18 distinct post-context states,
exclusively on the negative side. The broader organised-group/activity family
appears in all six orders across all 28 post-context states.

**P1-E — process / comparison / change**

Manipulation, process/processes, experimentation, extraction, creating,
comparing, change and interaction vocabulary recurs on the negative side.

### P1 relations

The dominant positive relation is sensing/instrumentation ↔ built
place/locality. Spatial/measurement ↔ organised group/activity is strong on
the negative side, and organised group/activity ↔ process/comparison/change is
also persistent.

Unlike R014's later comparison baseline, R015 first-pass evidence also shows a
substantial positive relation between sensing/place and modification/change
vocabulary. This observation is retained without forcing it into a prior map.

## P2 — sensor | apartment | sessions

Held out: `line`.

### Positive-side parents

**P2-A — sensing / instrumentation**

Sensor, sensors, laser, signal, electrodes and related technical vocabulary
persist across all six orders.

**P2-B — built place / locality**

Apartment, shop and named-place vocabulary form a persistent positive branch.

**P2-C — spatial / measurement structure**

The spatial family is reconstructed despite the absence of `line`, although
literal recovery is weak in the published community summaries. Literal
`line` appears in four of six permutations across two post-context states.
The broader line/area/space/field/position/size family appears in all six
orders across 26 distinct post-context states and spans both sectors.

**P2-F — reporting / information**

Report, output/file, view and related information vocabulary recurs as a
secondary positive branch.

**P2-G — modification / transformation**

Enlargement, transformations, decreasing and removal vocabulary is recurrent
on the positive side.

### Negative-side parents

**P2-D — organised group / activity**

Clubs, sessions, groups, activities, FIFA, tennis and related material persist
strongly on the negative side.

**P2-E — process / comparison / change**

Manipulation, process, experimentation, extraction, creating, comparing and
change vocabulary recur beside the group/activity and spatial families.

### P2 relations

Sensing/instrumentation ↔ built place/locality is again the strongest positive
relation. Spatial/measurement ↔ organised group/activity and organised
group/activity ↔ process/comparison/change are persistent negative relations.

P2 provides the weakest literal held-out recovery of R015 but still reconstructs
the held-out semantic family across every order. The distinction between
literal graph completion and family-level graph completion is therefore
important.

## P3 — sensor | line | sessions

Held out: `apartment`.

### Positive-side parents

**P3-A — sensing / instrumentation**

Laser, sensor, signal and microphone/apparatus vocabulary remain dominant.

**P3-B — built place / locality**

Although apartment is absent from the prompt, locality reappears through shop,
Bronx and other named/built-place vocabulary. Literal `apartment` appears in
four of six permutations across 12 post-context states, on the positive side.
The broader locality family appears in all six orders across all 28
post-context states.

**P3-C — spatial / measurement structure**

Line/area/space/size and related structural terms recur, with stronger
persistence on the negative side.

**P3-F — reporting / information**

Report, file/retrieve and related information vocabulary recurs.

**P3-G — modification / transformation**

Enlargement, decreasing, transformation/removal material recurs on the
positive side.

### Negative-side parents

**P3-D — organised group / activity**

FIFA, clubs, group/activity and related social-institutional material persist.

**P3-E — process / comparison / change**

Manipulation is especially persistent, accompanied by process, extraction,
creating, comparing, experimentation and change vocabulary.

### P3 relations

The same positive sensing/locality relation and negative
spatial/group/process chain remain recognisable despite apartment being held
out. P3 remains the most order-sensitive region at the measured graph level,
so parent continuity should not be mistaken for token-level invariance.

## P4 — apartment | line | sessions

Held out: `sensor`.

### Positive-side parents

**P4-A — sensing / instrumentation**

This is the strongest literal held-out reconstruction in R015. Literal
`sensor` appears in all six permutations across 21 post-context states,
exclusively on the positive side in the community summaries. Laser, sensors,
signal, wavelength, microphone, pumps and other instrumentation terms expand
the family. The broader family appears across all six orders and all 28
post-context states.

**P4-B — built place / locality**

Apartment, shop, house and named-place vocabulary form a persistent positive
branch.

**P4-C — spatial / measurement structure**

Line/area/space/size and related structural vocabulary recurs, predominantly
on the negative side.

**P4-F — reporting / information**

Report, file/output, write and related representation vocabulary recurs as a
secondary positive branch.

**P4-G — modification / transformation**

Enlargement, decreasing, removal and related change vocabulary is recurrent on
the positive side.

### Negative-side parents

**P4-D — organised group / activity**

Clubs, activities, groups, FIFA, tennis and related organised-activity material
persist strongly.

**P4-E — process / comparison / change**

Manipulation, process, extraction, creating, comparing and change material
recur beside the spatial and group/activity branches.

### P4 relations

Sensing/instrumentation ↔ built place/locality remains strong even though
sensor is held out. Spatial/measurement ↔ organised group/activity is strongest
here among the four R015 probes, while group/activity ↔ process/change remains
persistent.

This cross-sector breadth is retained as an R015-specific property rather than
being forced into a negative-only interpretation.

|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | sessions | 6/6 orders; 18 post-context states; negative only | 6/6 orders; all 28 post-context states |
| P2 | line | 4/6 orders; 2 post-context states; negative in literal summaries | 6/6 orders; 26 post-context states; both sectors |

| P4 | sensor | 6/6 orders; 21 post-context states; positive | 6/6 orders; all 28 post-context states |

R015 therefore supports family-level semantic graph completion in every probe,
but literal graph completion is uneven. P2 and P3 are weaker literal cases than
P1 and P4.

# Interpretation boundary

These parent labels and support values are downstream semantic
reconstructions. They are not raw model variables, and the published
community-summary audit samples representative/extreme community vocabulary
rather than every selected token membership.

The R015 map can now be constructed independently. Only after that map is
frozen should its families and relations be aligned with R014.
