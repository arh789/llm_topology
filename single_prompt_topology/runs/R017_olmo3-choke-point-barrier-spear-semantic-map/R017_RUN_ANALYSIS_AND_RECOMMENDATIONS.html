R017 analysis and recommendations

Measurement status: complete and validated. Semantic status: independently reconstructed. Invariance status: not tested.

1. Strongest supported result

For OLMo 3 32B Base, “choke point + collapsible barrier + spear” forms a coherent single-trajectory topology of controlled passage and tactical interception: geometry constrains movement, a deployable obstruction shapes the approach, and reach/piercing force acts at the resulting point of contact.

The model does not retain the prompt merely as three neighbouring nouns. Early and middle layers concretise the spear as a weapon and piercing object. Later layers increasingly preserve the functions joining the three inputs: point, intersection, narrow, flow, movement, folding, blocking, deployment, wall, restriction, attack and guard.

8. Sector asymmetry

The positive side increasingly carries the active procedure: weapon use, blocking, folding, movement, deployment, intersection, attack and guards. The negative side increasingly carries the spatial and limiting counterpart: route, exit, access, walls, restriction, channel and defence.

This is not evidence of suppression. Negative-tail means anti-alignment with the evolving anchor representation in the fixed embedding basis.

10. What R017 does not establish

  • No order invariance: only one permutation was measured.
  • No context invariance: the third-concept context was not varied.
  • No neighbouring-concept continuity: there is no R015-style adjacent-region substitution.
  • No cross-model invariance.
  • No causal internal variables: parent labels remain descriptive reconstructions.
  • No direct training-document recovery.
