# R014 independent probe reconstructions

## Purpose

Record the downstream semantic reconstruction of P1-P4 before cross-probe matching. R013 parent labels were not imposed as probe-local answers. Parent names below were assigned from recurrent R014 community content; cross-probe correspondence was evaluated only after the four reconstructions were complete.

The measured sources are the completed R014 community, edge, transition and permutation-invariance artefacts. No model forward pass, cutoff change, graph reconstruction or community re-detection was performed.

## P1 — laser | city | spatial field

### Positive-side parents

**P1-A — technical sensing / apparatus**

Recurring community material includes laser, sensor, sensors, electrodes, signal, device, batteries, pumps, optical/technical objects and related physical apparatus. This is the dominant positive lineage.

**P1-B — built / situated environment**

Recurring material includes city, kitchen, apartment, shop, named cities and regions, country/place vocabulary and built-environment terms.

**P1-C — spatial / measurement organisation**

Recurring material includes field, area, line, space, parameters, weighting, statistical, measure, polygon, size and directional terms. It overlaps the positive technical/place organisation and also appears independently on the negative side.

**P1-F — representation / reporting**

Report, data, image/text/system and operational vocabulary recur, but this parent is less central than P1-A/P1-B/P1-C and becomes especially explicit near the output-facing state.

### Negative-side parents

**P1-D — social / group activity**

Clubs, sessions, groups, activities, teams, exhibitions, sport and related social/institutional material form a persistent negative-side lineage.

This is a held-out reconstruction: clubs is not in P1. The literal club lexeme appears in community summaries in all six P1 permutations, across 24 post-context states (S05-S28), exclusively on the negative side.

**P1-E — interaction / change**

Manipulation, interactions, comparisons, transformations, distribution, fragmentation, change and process vocabulary repeatedly organise alongside P1-D and P1-C.

### P1 relations

The strongest recurring positive relation is P1-A ↔ P1-B, with P1-C attached to both. The strongest recurring negative relation is P1-C ↔ P1-D, followed by P1-D ↔ P1-E.

# Held-out reconstruction test

Each probe omits one of the four seed concepts. The missing semantic region is nevertheless reconstructed from the remaining three:

| Probe | Held-out seed | Post-context recovery |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | clubs | literal clubs in 6/6 permutations; 24 states; negative-side social field persistent |
| P2 | spatial field | field/area in 6/6 permutations; all 28 post-context states; spatial field spans both sectors |
| P3 | city | literal city in 5/6 permutations; broader place/environment field strong in positive topology |
| P4 | laser | literal laser in 6/6 permutations; 26 states; positive technical field present in ~95% of S06-S27 networks |

None of laser, city, field, area or clubs appears in the common State-00 community summaries. Their held-out recurrence is therefore post-context rather than a property of the identical pre-context anchor state.

This constitutes semantic graph-completion evidence: neighbouring concepts can reconstruct a missing region of the same learned semantic neighbourhood.

# Cross-probe relation recurrence

After parent matching, relation recurrence was audited over S06-S27. A relation was counted as instantiated when the two reconstructed families occurred either within one community or in distinct communities joined by an inter-community edge. These support values are downstream semantic diagnostics, not original R014 measured metrics.

| Relation | Sector | P1 | P2 | P3 | P4 | Mean |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| technical ↔ place | positive | .86 | .80 | .83 | .77 | .82 |
| technical ↔ spatial | positive | .48 | .58 | .60 | .52 | .54 |
| place ↔ spatial | positive | .43 | .48 | .48 | .41 | .45 |
| spatial ↔ social | negative | .89 | .72 | .82 | .80 | .81 |
| social ↔ interaction | negative | .42 | .48 | .44 | .64 | .50 |
| spatial ↔ interaction | negative | .37 | .36 | .38 | .49 | .40 |
| technical ↔ representation | positive | .36 | .42 | .35 | .33 | .37 |

The graph is not complete. Direct technical-social, technical-interaction and place-interaction relations are substantially weaker than the relations above.

The resulting topology is therefore structured rather than a generic co-activation of all recovered parents.
