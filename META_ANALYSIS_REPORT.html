Controlling interpretation. The metaanalysis is not primarily a chronology of runs. The runs form an evidence chain beneath the central research question: whether controlled changes in transformer-state representations can reveal higher-order semantic topology learned from the training distribution.

4. Semantic epochs

EpochApprox. entries / runsDominant questionStructural change
1. Measurement formationMA-0001–0011 · R001–R004What can actually be measured across transformer depth?Hidden states, vocabulary projection, similarity curves, cut-offs and curvature become the measurement instrument.
2. Topology formationMA-0012–0028 · R005–R009Do selected semantic populations form meaningful network structure?Tokens become networks; positive/negative populations, communities and state-local topology replace simple nearest-neighbour lists.
3. Latent structure reconstructionMA-0029–0054 · R009–R010What higher-order structure generates the observed communities?The unit of analysis moves from community to parent structure. Layer-wise semantic evolution becomes the real object.
4. Generative-role theoryMA-0055–0064How should those parent structures be interpreted functionally?SDA-3 expands into GRD: generative, boundary, adjacent, emerging, decaying, residual, conflict, coherence and resolution. The analysis becomes relational rather than topical.
5. Controlled semantic perturbationMA-0065–0077 · R012–R013Which structures survive changes in presentation?Prompt order is deliberately perturbed. The stable object becomes not a state, but what survives transformations of states.
6. Semantic cartographyMA-0078–0098 · R013–R015Can invariant structures from separate probes be joined into a larger map?One triplet stops being the semantic object. Overlapping and neighbouring probes produce local semantic topology and a possible method for traversing semantic space.
7. Active structural inferenceMA-0099–0101What can be inferred from what is present, missing, emerging or unexpectedly correlated?SDA-3 becomes operational: Emerging is a discovery operator, Suppressed a missing-node test, and HCU an anti-interpretive correlation probe. Cartography begins becoming active inference.
8. Hypothesis reformulation + metacognitionMA-0082–0103, especially MA-0083 / MA-0099 / MA-0100What is the project actually doing, and how is the method itself being learned?“Infer training data” is narrowed to inferring higher-order semantic regularities learned from the training distribution. A second research object appears: the human–LLM learning loop itself.
9. Scale transfer and independent validationMA-0104–0113 · R016–R018Does the method survive a larger model and stricter perturbation?OLMo 3 32B becomes viable; measurement and interpretation are formally separated; R018 shows strong order-conditioned divergence followed by higher-order reconvergence.

5. Interpretation

The metaanalysis records two coupled discoveries. The first is the experimental method for extracting increasingly stable semantic invariants from transformer-state change under controlled perturbation. The second is the procedure by which the research method itself evolved: candidate structure is generated, premature coherence and abstraction drift are challenged, unresolved evidence is preserved, and only structures that survive repeated adversarial reconstruction are retained.

The resulting project-level object is therefore not any single state, token set, community, prompt, or run. It is the topology that survives transformations of those observations.
