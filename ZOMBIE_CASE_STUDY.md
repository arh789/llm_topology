# Behavioural Case Mapping: Zombie Survival by ChatGPT

## Source and status

Source: [Zombie Survival by ChatGPT — Why the AI Lies (and How to Stop It)](https://decrepitfilth.art/art/zombie-survival-chatgpt-why-the-ai-lies-and-how-to-stop-it)

The page records a human-supervised sequence in which ChatGPT moves from a
genre-shaped zombie-survival answer through contradiction, branch rejection,
reconstruction and convergence on a smaller strategy.

Treat it as a behavioural precursor and source of experimental hypotheses. It
is not evidence for a latent geometric or topological mechanism.

## Relationship to the five research questions

| Research question | Behavioural case | Unresolved issue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where is coherence most stable? | Scarcity, mobility, modularity, temporary fortification and controlled engagement geometry repeatedly survive reconstruction. | The recurrence may reflect structural stability, conversational priming or researcher steering. |
| Where is coherence least stable? | Bioweapons, permanent fortresses, fuel-dependent mobility, hybrid weapons and rebuilding collapse under pressure. | Disappearance may indicate contradiction recognition, prompt compliance or deliberate pruning. |
| What addition A increases coherence? | The protection–entrapment contradiction is reconstructed into temporary, modular fortification. | The case does not independently establish that all contradictory evidence remained represented. |
| What addition B conceals contradiction? | Biological, firearm and symbolic branches are explicitly suppressed. | The case does not distinguish justified removal of irrelevant material from concealment of inconvenient evidence. |
| What trajectory follows A versus B? | The page preserves a long sequence of collapse, re-emergence, suppression and convergence. | It does not run matched A and B trajectories from an identical starting state. |

## Strongest supported relationship

The case distinguishes a fluent answer from relationships that continue to
appear after assumptions are challenged. This is a useful behavioural analogue
of coherence stability under perturbation.

The proposed topology project would test whether that observable stability has
a reproducible internal representational correlate and whether the correlate
causally affects later responses.

## Key limitations and extrapolations

### Single-case generalisation

One long interaction with an unspecified ChatGPT version cannot establish a
general property of language models. Multiple cases, paraphrases, seeds, model
families and training stages are required.

### No matched A-versus-B condition

The interventions are adaptive and cumulative. There is no controlled point at
which identical starting contexts receive matched evidence-preserving and
evidence-concealing interventions followed by identical questions.

### Researcher steering is confounded with model discovery

The human selects branches, rejects interpretations, introduces external
analogies and suppresses recurring concepts. Convergence may therefore measure
the joint human–model search process rather than autonomous model recognition.

### Pressure stability may be conversational persistence

Re-emergence can result from repetition, accumulated context, lexical priming
or inference about the user's preference. Fresh-context replications,
counterbalanced ordering and controlled context ablations are needed.

### Semantic categories are not measured geometry

The page's central, suppressed, adjacent and emerging categories describe
generated responses. Its combat geometry is physical engagement geometry.
Neither is a measurement of activation distance, neighbourhood structure,
topology or curvature.

### Model self-explanation is a hypothesis

Retrospective explanations about option preservation, coherence pressure,
training associations, preference shaping and safety shaping are outputs to
test, not privileged access to hidden computation.

### Convergence is not truth

A narrower response can be more internally consistent without being more
accurate. Historical analogy does not establish an objectively optimal zombie
strategy, and the topology project cannot supply real-world zombie ground
truth.

### No causal evidence

Prompt changes followed by response changes do not show that a particular
internal representation caused recognition or concealment. Activation patching,
ablation or an equivalent intervention is required.

### Reproducibility is incomplete

The model revision, system prompt, decoding configuration and seed are not
specified. A reproducible style of questioning is not yet a reproducible
experimental result.

## Candidate controlled stimulus

The page's fortification conflict can be converted into a first experimental
case:

1. **Baseline:** Fixed fortification provides protection from a numerically
   superior horde.
2. **Conflict:** Fixed fortification also increases predictability and the risk
   of entrapment.
3. **Addition A candidate:** Require both protection and rapid abandonment to
   remain explicit constraints.
4. **Addition B candidate:** Add an assumption that discounts or removes the
   entrapment evidence while leaving the answer apparently coherent.
5. **Trajectory:** Ask the same follow-up questions after A and B.
6. **Measurements:** Score contradiction acknowledgement and evidence
   retention; capture specified hidden states; compare distances and
   neighbourhood stability; reserve causal claims for later interventions.

The wording and lengths of A and B must be matched, the condition labels must
be neutral, and the logical status of the evidence must be established before
the model is evaluated.

## Falsification value

If controlled A and B conditions do not produce reproducibly different
behavioural or internal signatures, the case can remain useful as a
human-guided reasoning workflow without supporting a stronger claim about
internal semantic topology.
